# Pasha — tone of voice

**Language:** Indonesian (`id-ID`) with a few intentional English field-note labels as graphic ephemera.  
**Family:** `casual-friendly` with a grounded professional undercurrent.  
**Audience:** internship recruiters, collaborators and people who want to get to know Pasha's capabilities.  
**Pronouns:** friendly first-person “aku” only in the introductory editorial voice; substantive professional claims are in third person and tied to the CV.  
**Status:** **editorial-draft**, not client-confirmed.  
**Sources:** user instruction for santai/natural; verified CV; supplied photography and original artwork.

**Prefer:** “kenalan”, “belajar”, “mencoba”, “dibuat”, “dirajut”, “yuk ngobrol”, “perjalanan”, “banyak hal seru”.  
**Avoid:** “passionate professional driven by excellence”, fake client quotations, made-up metrics, exaggerated claims of mastery, misleading “brand founder” titles, overly slangy or corporate pitch text.  
**Rhythm:** short natural headings, two- to three-sentence source-backed descriptive units, no hollow poetic sentences in the professional chronology.

## Same-site samples

**Opening:** “Hai, aku Pasha. Lagi belajar banyak hal—dari dapur, ide usaha, sampai karya-karya kecil yang dibuat dengan tangan sendiri.”

**Evidence:** “Lewat Savorita, Pasha pernah menjalankan usaha kue kering dengan putri salju sebagai produk utama. Dari produksi, pengemasan, sampai mengatur pesanan, semuanya jadi pengalaman belajar yang nyata.”

**Action:** “Kalau ada kesempatan belajar, berkolaborasi, atau sekadar ingin bertukar cerita, kabari Pasha, ya.”

## Review notes

Professional CV data are not put in the mouth of the client as fabricated quotations. Site mentions no particular trip, employment at the photographed location, duration of any crochet commission, or specific achievements beyond the CV. Button labels share friendly wording across the page. The entire tone remains editorial until Pasha reviews it.
